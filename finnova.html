Our Story

Built by people tired of guessing where their money went

FinNova started in 2021 when three former bank engineers got frustrated juggling five different apps just to understand their own finances. What began as a personal spreadsheet grew into a platform now trusted by millions.

Today, FinNova connects banking, budgeting, investing, and business finance into one secure, intelligent experience — without ever selling user data.

Our Mission

Make confident financial decisions accessible to everyone, regardless of income or background.

Our Vision
